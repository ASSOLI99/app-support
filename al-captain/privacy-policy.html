1 1. Introduction

Welcome to Al-Captain, a ride-booking and transportation app connecting passengers with drivers. Assoli Industries is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information. By using the app, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2 2. Data We Collect

Right Description
Account & Profile Name, phone, gender, profile picture, UUID, user type, email
Location Current location, trip start/end points, saved addresses
Trip & Booking Booking details, ride history, ride status, driver/passenger info
Financial Subscription info, transaction history, pricing data
Device & Usage Notifications, session tokens, locally cached data

3 3. How We Use Your Data

  • Service delivery — connecting passengers with drivers, processing bookings, displaying maps
  • Authentication — sign-in via Google or Apple
  • Notifications — sending updates about trips and bookings
  • App improvement — analyzing usage patterns to improve the service
  • Safety & security — preventing fraud and ensuring user safety
  • Customer support — responding to help requests

4 4. Third-Party Sharing

Party Purpose
Supabase Database and authentication
Google Maps / Places Map display and location suggestions
Google OAuth Sign-in
Apple Authentication iOS sign-in
Twilio Sending verification messages (OTP)
Expo Notifications App push notifications
We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5 5. Permissions Requested

Permission Reason
Location Set your pickup point and help the driver reach you
Microphone In-app voice communication features
Photo Library Uploading a profile picture or vehicle information
Notifications Trip alerts and booking updates

6 6. Data Storage & Security

  • Data stored on encrypted Supabase servers
  • Session data stored locally using secure storage (AsyncStorage)
  • All data transfers use HTTPS
  • Authentication tokens are automatically refreshed

7 7. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active
  • Trip history: retained for operational and dispute resolution purposes
  • Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days

8 8. Your Rights

Right Description
Access View your personal data stored with us
Correct Fix any inaccurate information
Delete Remove your account and all associated data
Opt Out Unsubscribe from notifications at any time
Object Dispute processing of your data for certain purposes

To exercise these rights, contact us at: abdullah.asoully@gmail.com

9 9. Children's Privacy

Al-Captain is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10 10. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified of material changes via an in-app notification or email. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
