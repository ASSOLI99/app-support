Who We Are

Built by people who love to move

Al-Captain was founded by Assoli Industries with a mission to reimagine how people get around. We believe every ride should be comfortable, predictable, and affordable.

Our platform connects passengers with a network of verified, professional drivers — bringing transparency and trust to every trip.

From our first beta to thousands of completed rides, we continue to build features driven by real user feedback.
